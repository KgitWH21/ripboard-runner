# Ripboard Runner

Race your way through Yuba City, 2062 to beat your rival Harpreet. A browser game set in the world of **Cereus & Limnic** (Chapters 3 and 4).

**Play:** https://kgitwh21.github.io/ripboard-runner/

- Five story stages with cutscenes, medals, hidden lore stars and credits
- Speedrun mode: the whole campaign on one clock, with splits and a leaderboard
- Cell power levels: every few cells raises your top speed
- A secret bonus stage: San Francisco streets at twilight (finish the main run to unlock)
- Soundtrack: Cereus & Limnic tracks by Keith Hayden, streamed from keithhayden.net

Controls: arrows / A-D or swipe to switch lanes, Up / W / Space or swipe up to jump, Shift / E / Down or tap Boost. M toggles music, N skips the track, P or Esc pauses.

`index.html` is the whole game in one file. `tools/` and `src/` hold the build scripts and medal tuning that produced it (they expect the original working files and are kept for reference).
